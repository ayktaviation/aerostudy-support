Privacy Policy

AeroStudy Pilot and AeroStudy Technician · Last updated 19 November 2026

The short version

These apps collect nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your answers, scores, session history, bookmarks and any knowledge-test codes you type or import are written to the app's own storage on your device. They are never uploaded. If you delete the app, they are gone.

Microphone and speech

The voice tutor is optional. When you use it, iOS transcribes your speech so the app can compare it with the reference answer. Recognition is performed on the device and no recording or transcript is stored or transmitted by the app. If you never open the voice tutor, the microphone is never used.

Documents you import

You can import a photo or PDF of a knowledge-test report. The file is read on your device to extract the codes and is not copied or uploaded anywhere.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether the unlock is active. The developer never sees your name, payment details or Apple Account.

Children

The apps are rated 4+ and collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
