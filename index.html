Frequently asked

How does the free version work?

The first 50 questions of every subject are free, with their figures. One payment unlocks the whole bank, the timed practice exams and the voice tutor. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

I paid before and reinstalled — how do I get it back?

Open the unlock screen and tap Restore a previous purchase. The unlock belongs to your Apple Account, so it also comes back on your other devices signed in to the same account.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. Questions, figures and your progress are bundled in the app and stay on your device. The only time the App Store is contacted is when you buy or restore the unlock.

Where do the questions and figures come from?

Figures come from the FAA Airman Knowledge Testing Supplements, which are U.S. government works in the public domain. Explanations are drawn from the FAA handbooks, advisory circulars and 14 CFR, with the source named under each answer.

Is this an official FAA test?

No. AeroStudy is practice material. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FAA, and it does not replace your instructor or the current regulations.

I think an answer or a figure is wrong.

Please write with the question text and what you believe the correct answer is. Corrections go into the next update.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything it stored, because nothing is kept anywhere else.
